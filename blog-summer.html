5 Tips for Summer Pet Care

May 15, 2026 • By Dr. Patnaik

Patna heats up fast in the summer! Just like us, our pets need extra care when the temperature rises. Here are 5 essential tips to keep your furry friends cool and safe.

1. Hydration is Key 💧

Always keep fresh, cool water available. Pets dehydrate faster than humans. Consider adding a few ice cubes to their water bowl for a refreshing treat.

2. Avoid Mid-Day Walks ☀️

The pavement can get scorching hot and burn your pet's paws. Walk them early in the morning or late in the evening. Test the ground with your palm—if it's too hot for you, it's too hot for them.

3. Never Leave Pets in Cars 🚗

Even with windows cracked, a car can become an oven in minutes. This is dangerous and can be fatal. Leave them at home in the AC/fan if you're running errands.

4. Summer Grooming ✂️

While shaving isn't always good (fur protects from sun!), a good trim and de-shedding session helps air circulate to their skin. Visit Patna Pets for a professional summer cut!

5. Watch for Heatstroke ⚠️

Signs include heavy panting, drooling, lethargy, and vomiting. If you suspect heatstroke, wrap them in wet towels and rush to a vet immediately.
