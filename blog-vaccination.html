PET HEALTH

Importance of Regular Vaccination

June 12, 2026 • By Dr. Patnaik

Vaccination is the single most effective way to protect your pet from deadly diseases. It's not just a medical procedure; it's an act of love.

1. Prevents Deadly Diseases 🦠

Vaccines protect against Parvovirus, Distemper, Hepatitis, and Rabies. These diseases can be fatal and are expensive to treat.

2. It's the Law ⚖️

In many places, including Patna, the Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) is mandatory for pets. It protects your pet, your family, and the community.

3. The Schedule 📅

  • Puppies/Kittens: Start at 6-8 weeks. Boosters every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks.
  • Adults: Annual boosters are usually required (DHPP, Rabies).

4. Boarding Requirements 🎟️

If you plan to use our boarding services, your pet MUST be up-to-date on vaccinations. This ensures a safe environment for all guests.
