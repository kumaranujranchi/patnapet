Choosing the Right Food for Your Dog

June 02, 2026 • By Nutritionist Riya

With so many options in the market (Royal Canin, Pedigree, home-cooked), picking the right food can be confusing. Here is a simple guide to help you decide.

1. Age Matters 👶👴

Puppies need high-protein food for growth, while senior dogs need lower calorie food to maintain weight. Always check the label for "Puppy", "Adult", or "Senior".

2. Breed Size 🐕

A German Shepherd has different needs than a Pug. Large breeds need joint support, while small breeds need smaller kibble size for easier chewing.

3. Check Ingredients 🥩

Look for meat (chicken, lamb) as the first ingredient. Avoid fillers like corn and soy if your dog has allergies. Grain-free isn't always better—consult your vet!

4. Home Food vs. Packaged 🍲

Home food is great but often lacks specific nutrients like calcium and taurine. If you feed home-cooked meals, add vet-approved supplements.
